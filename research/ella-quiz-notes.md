# Ella Verbs: quizzes, levels and progress (observed 2026-11-06, Android, free tier)

Screenshots: `research/shots/20-*` to `68-*`. Video: `research/video/ella-03.mp4`.
Quizzes were answered by `research/ella.js`, which reads the prompt over adb and answers
with Conjuga's own conjugation engine.

## Free vs Pro
- Free: present tense and the Top 100 verbs. Pro: all 18 tenses, 2,500+ verbs,
  Smart Quizzes, per-verb quizzes, cheat sheets, saving custom quizzes.
- Paywall after a level's quizzes: Annual £54.99/yr (£4.58/mo), Monthly £8.99/mo.
  A second screen offers a bonus "Verb Reference Guide" for annual before a deadline.

## Home path
- 41 levels and 10 milestones. Order: Beginner 1–3, M1 Presente basics; Presente regulars,
  stem changes 1–2, irregular yo, irregulars 1–2, M2; reflexive verbs, presente progresivo 1–2,
  futuro próximo, pretérito perfecto (+ irregular participles 1–2), M3; pretérito indefinido
  regulars, irregular yo, stem changes 1–2, irregulars 1–3, M4; imperfecto (+ irregulars),
  pluscuamperfecto, M5; futuro simple (+ irregulars), futuro perfecto, condicional simple,
  condicional perfecto, M6; presente de subjuntivo, stem changes 1–2, irregulars, M7;
  imperfecto de subjuntivo, perfecto subj., pluscuamperfecto subj., M8; imperativo,
  irregulars 1–2, M9; M10 overall recap.
- Each level card: Learn (Usage, Formation, Verb vocab), Practice (AR / ER / IR quizzes,
  Custom practice), Vocabulary (Flash cards, a "Regular verbs" meaning quiz), Level up.
  Completed quizzes get a trophy badge.
- Usage lesson: tense description + example sentence table (Spanish | English).
- Formation lesson: "verb stem + endings" table by pronoun for AR/ER/IR, then a worked
  example table (hablar, beber, vivir) with English under each form.
- Level up: 30-question test, pass with 28+. Result: "LEVEL UP · Level 5 unlocked",
  "Your path: 6 of 51 levels", tenses covered, verbs covered.

## Conjugation quiz (typing)
- Header: Exit, "n / 10", ✓ count, ✗ count, settings gear. Progress bar.
- Prompt: tense chip, "Tú (DEJAR)", English gloss "you leave (behind)".
- Answer field with á é í ó ú ñ keys and an enter key.
- Correct: advances straight away (no review screen by default).
- Near miss: "There might be a typo…" on the first try; submitting again counts it.
- Wrong: bottom sheet "That's incorrect…" showing the right form with the differing letters
  coloured, your answer with its wrong letters coloured, the English, and a diagnosis such as
  "Right verb, wrong tense. That is the subjunctive present, but this one was looking for the
  present tense." plus a short note on the tense. Buttons: Steps, Next question.
- Results: big %, "El Presente – 9 of 10 correct", message, streak chip, "Your mistakes"
  (form, right vs wrong, steps button, "1 new in Smart Quiz – repeated until mastered"),
  "Verbs covered" with stars. Buttons: Continue, Quiz revisions (or Share on 100%).

## Other quiz types (Custom Quiz tab)
- What to practise: Conjugations | Sentences | Meanings. Tenses, Pronouns (yo, tú,
  ella/él/usted, nosotros, ellos/ustedes; Randomise, Select all), Verbs (Top 100 …).
- How: Typing | Multiple choice | Flash cards | Speaking. Questions: 10 with −5 / +5.
- Make it harder: Listening only, Hide infinitive, Hide English, Spanish pronoun + English
  verb, Irregular conjugations only. Saved quizzes. Link to a Verb Sudoku hub.
- Sentences: "(ESCRIBIR) en mi diario todas las mañanas antes de desayunar." with the English
  sentence underneath, the subject and verb in bold ("**I write**").
- Meanings, multiple choice: English meaning, four options: the answer, a real verb, and
  made-up verbs with the wrong ending (probir, intenter, descubrar). Wrong answer sheet shows
  what the chosen verb means and "We would have also accepted: …" synonyms.
- Flash cards: list of the deck's verbs first, then English → "Reveal answer" → Spanish,
  audio and synonyms → "I was incorrect" / "I was correct".

## In-quiz settings
Theme, sound, Spanish type (Latin American), Spanish tense names, Auto-check answers,
Check accents (off by default), Check for typos (on), Pause on correct answer
(Never / On synonyms / Always), Include all verbs.
