# Ella Verbs: verb dictionary (observed 2026-11-06, Android)

Screenshots: `research/shots/02-*` to `12-*`. Video: `research/video/ella-02.mp4`.

## List screen (Verbs tab)
- Search field: "Search (verb or conjugation)". Typing a conjugated form ("fui") shows
  an "Exact conjugation matches" section (ir, ser).
- Segmented control: All (locked, Pro) | Top 100 | My Verbs (locked, Pro).
- Filter button opens "Verb filters" sheet:
  - Verb: All verbs | My Verbs (n)
  - Popularity: Top 100 / 200 / 300 / 500 / 1000 / All verbs, plus a from-to rank slider
  - Verb types: All | ER | IR | AR
  - Reflexive verbs: Exclude | Include | Only
  - Irregularity filters (multi): All types, Regular, Irregular, Highly irregular,
    Stem changing, Irregular gerundio, Irregular past participle
  - Footer: live "150+ verbs match your filters", Reset, Save
- Rows: star (favourite), infinitive, English meanings, IRREGULAR tag, chevron.
  Grouped by letter with an A–Z rail on the right (rail ends in a search icon).
- Includes reflexive entries as separate verbs (abrir / abrirse).

## Verb detail screen
- Header: infinitive + speaker, meaning, tags (Irregular (i), Top 100),
  "Quiz <verb>" (Pro), star, gear. Flag icon in app bar (report issue).
- Gerundio and Participio rows with English ("opening", "opened").
- Grouped by mood, each with a one-line description and a Quiz button:
  - Indicative tenses: Presente, Futuro Próximo, Pretérito Indefinido, Imperfecto, Futuro,
    Condicional; compound: Perfecto, Pluscuamperfecto, Futuro Perfecto, (Condicional Perfecto)
  - Subjunctive: Presente, Imperfecto, Futuro de Subjuntivo (collapsed); compound:
    Pretérito Perfecto de Subjuntivo, Pluscuamperfecto
  - Imperative: affirmative and negative (no yo row)
- Each tense card: (i) info, Quiz, collapse chevron.
- Each form row: pronoun + form + speaker, English translation underneath, and a
  graduation-cap button.
- Irregular letters are coloured red (ab**iert**o).
- Ends with numbered "Example sentences" (Spanish + English).

## Sheets
- Graduation cap: "Conjugation steps", e.g. "Yo (ABRIR) in the subjunctive present: abra":
  1. take the yo present form and drop its last letter, 2. add the ending, 3. combine.
  Buttons: Flag issue, Close.
- Tense (i): Spanish name, English name, Usage paragraph, numbered examples,
  Cheat sheet (Pro), Upgrade to quiz (Pro).
- Gear, "Verb library settings": Hide Spanish pronouns, Show tenses side-by-side,
  Expand all by default, Include vosotros, Include usted, Use Spanish tense names.
